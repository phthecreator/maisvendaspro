# Task: Generate Daily Social Content

## Objective
Transform researched trends into engaging social media posts for LinkedIn and Meta.

## Instructions
1. **Input**: Receive a `trends_report.md` from the @Scout.
2. **Drafting**: Create 3 post variations using the "Vibe Coding" tone (Professional yet Hacker/Modern).
3. **Style Check**: Coordinate with @Style to ensure the post reflects the personal brand authority.
4. **Finalizing**: Format the post with appropriate hashtags and emojis.

## Output
- `content_drafts_{date}.md`
