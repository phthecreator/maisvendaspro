# Marketing & Branding Standards

## Brand Voice
- **Tone**: Professional, Disruptive, Visionary.
- **Language**: Portuguese (BR) with technical terms preserved (Vibe Coding, LLM, etc.).
- **Visuals**: Dark mode, Neon accents, Terminal aesthetics.

## Posting Rules
- **Frequency**: Daily (Monday-Friday).
- **Time Slots**: 10:00 AM (Engagement) and 6:00 PM (Direct Sales/CTA).
- **Approval**: User must approve post drafts before final "ready-to-post" status.
