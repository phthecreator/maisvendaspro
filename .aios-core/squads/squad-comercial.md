## 🎤 PITCH FRAMEWORK (SPIN Method)

### Situation
"I see you're a [marketing agency / SaaS / etc] dealing with [specific pain: lead response time, scaling challenges]"

### Problem
"How are you currently handling [problem]? What's the impact on your revenue?"

### Implication
"If you could automate that, how much more revenue could you generate? What would it be worth to you?"

### Need-Payoff
"So what you really need is a system that [solution], so you can [business outcome]. Does that make sense?"

## 📋 SALES PROCESS

```
STAGE 1: Discovery Call (30 min)
├─ Understand their pain
├─ Qualify if Specialist or Enterprise
└─ Share 2-3 relevant case studies

STAGE 2: Proposal (Email)
├─ Custom proposal (1-page)
├─ Timeline (7 or 30 days)
├─ Investment ($3k or $6k+)
└─ Next steps

STAGE 3: Negotiation (Call)
├─ Answer objections
├─ Adjust scope if needed
├─ Confirm timeline & budget
└─ Send contract

STAGE 4: Signed & Onboarded
├─ Kick-off meeting
├─ Set success metrics
├─ Weekly check-ins
└─ Delivery in 7 days
```
